SOUND FAMILIAR?

This session is for you if…

  • You open another app during a training and can’t find your way back to Zoom.
  • You copy an AI response, but it doesn’t paste into your email or document where you want it.
  • You download a file and don’t know where it went.
  • Someone says “open the app,” and you’re not sure where to find it.

Your Mac laptop or desktop, an internet connection, and a way to join Zoom. This session covers Mac computers; Windows, iPhone, and iPad navigation are not covered.

We’ll demonstrate each skill, pause for practice, and answer common questions. Individual computer repairs are outside the scope of this one-hour workshop.
