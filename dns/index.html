Connect Your Domain

Point the domain you bought in Week 2 at your live site. It's the one step that looks a little different for everyone — here's the map.

🥇 The one golden rule
Vercel tells you the exact values to enter. Your only job is to copy them into your domain company's DNS settings.
Don't copy the example values on this page — they change, and yours can be different. Always use the exact records shown in your Vercel dashboard. This page shows you where to put them.

First — what is this even doing?

Every website lives on a computer, and that computer has an address — its IP address (IP = Internet Protocol). It's just a string of numbers like 216.150.1.1. That's the real location of your site. But nobody wants to type numbers, so DNS is the internet's phone book: someone types your name, DNS finds the number.

Connecting a domain means adding two entries to that phone book:

RecordForWhat it does
A record
A = Address		your root domain
yourdomain.com		Points straight to a number (Vercel's IP). It's the actual address.
CNAME
Canonical Name		the www version
(and any subdomain: shop., app.)		A nickname — points to another name, not a number. "www is just another name for your site."

The line that sticks: A record points to a number. CNAME points to a name. Your root domain gets the real address; www and other subdomains are just nicknames pointing to it. (It's also a DNS rule: a root domain isn't allowed to be a CNAME — that's why root = A, www = CNAME.)

1 Get your values from Vercel

  1. Vercel → your project → Settings → Domains → Add Domain.
  2. Type your domain (e.g. yourdomain.com) → accept the prompt to also add www.yourdomain.com.
  3. Vercel now shows the records to add — roughly:
    • A record → Host @ → Value: an IP like 216.150.1.1 (older projects show 76.76.21.21 — use whatever yours says)
    • CNAME → Host www → Value: something like cname.vercel-dns.com or a project-specific xxxx.vercel-dns-017.com
📸 Screenshot this Vercel screen — it's your reference sheet for Step 2. Vercel keeps showing these records (with a live status) even after you're done, so you can always come back.

2 Add the records at your domain company

GoDaddy shown here — the idea is identical everywhere, only the buttons move.

  1. Log in → Domains → your domain → DNS / Manage DNS.
  2. Delete the conflicting defaults first (see warning below).
  3. Add the A record: Type A · Host @ · Value = the IP from Vercel · TTL default.
  4. Add the CNAME: Type CNAME · Host www · Value = the CNAME from Vercel · TTL default.
  5. Save.
⚠️ GoDaddy gotcha (the #1 "I did everything and it still doesn't work"): GoDaddy auto-creates a "Parked" A record on @ (and sometimes a www CNAME). These block your new ones. Delete GoDaddy's existing @ A record and any conflicting www CNAME before adding Vercel's.

3 Confirm it worked

  1. Back in Vercel → Settings → Domains, click Refresh → status should read "Valid Configuration."
  2. Visit yourdomain.com → your site loads. 🎉
  3. Not instant? Normal. DNS takes a few minutes up to 24–48 hours to spread (usually much faster). Track it at whatsmydns.net — enter your domain, pick "A," watch it go green worldwide.
✓ Done when: your site loads on your real domain, on Valid Configuration.
