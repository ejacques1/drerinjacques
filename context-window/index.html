Friday Live — The Basics

Your AI's working memory.

Why your AI forgets things in a long chat, and how much memory each one actually gives you, free vs. paid.

What it is

The context window.

  • How much the AI can hold in mind at once, in one conversation.
  • Measured in tokens. One token is about ¾ of a word.
Why it forgets

It fills up, then it drops the oldest parts.

  • Everything counts against it: your messages, its replies, files, instructions.
  • Long chat → it fills up → it starts summarizing or dropping the beginning.
  • That's why it "forgets." Not broken, just full.
  • The fix: start a fresh chat (and if you're building, save where you are first).

The takeaway

When your AI forgets, that's the window filling up, not a glitch. Start fresh, and pick the tool with the most room to think, especially on the free plan.
