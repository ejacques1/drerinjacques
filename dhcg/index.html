Ongoing

What it costs to run

Once everything is built out and adjusted to what you want, there is one ongoing cost: Supabase, the secure database behind the admin panel. It stores family accounts, student progress and your session history.

$25 a month — roughly $300 a year. There is a free tier, but it puts a project to sleep after a week without visitors, which would mean families finding a dead site between sessions. The paid tier keeps it always on.

Hosting may add about $20 a month depending on which plan applies. I'll confirm before anything is set up.
