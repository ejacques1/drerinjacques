"So what does an AI-powered web app actually look like inside?"

Every AI-powered web app has two layers.

The business layer has existed for years, login, database, billing, dashboard, notifications, admin, API connections. It's the foundation.

Then AI adds a second layer: the intelligence layer, agents, voice, chat, automation, and whatever comes next.

The business layer runs the business. The intelligence layer creates the magic.
