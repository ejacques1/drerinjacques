First, let's center on one idea.

Over our time together, I'm going to talk about a lot of things, agents, voice, chat, automation, APIs. It can feel like a pile of separate tools.

They're not separate tools. They're all components inside a web app.

Every app has two parts: the core components you already expect (login, billing, a database), and the AI components that make it smart (agents, voice, chat, automation). Put them together, and that's a web app people can subscribe to.

Pricing that converts.

Once you can take payments, how you show the price matters as much as the price itself. Good pricing UX:

  • Three tiers, and highlight the middle one. Start low, go high, and mark the one you want them to pick as "Most Popular." People anchor to the middle.
  • One clear, bold price per tier. Never make them hunt for it.
  • Lead with monthly. Add an annual option (with a discount) once you have traction.
  • Short benefit list, not a feature dump. Three or four lines per tier.
  • One consistent button on every tier (Subscribe / Start).
  • Reduce the risk. A free trial or money-back removes the "will this work for me?" fear.
  • Max three tiers. Too many choices freeze people.

Your questions this week.

Grouped into three, so one answer covers a whole cluster.

1. "I'm on a PC and hitting walls the video doesn't show."

Git isn't installed · "bash not recognized" · winget · "claude not recognized" · 2FA · GitHub Desktop on the C drive

The videos demo on a Mac. On Windows there are two or three different steps, install Git, use the Windows install command, set up 2FA. If you're a PC user hitting a wall, it's almost always one of those. You're not behind, and the install-commands doc in the classroom has exactly what to swap.

👉 The "claude is not recognized" one, in plain terms: when Claude installs a command-line tool, it drops it in a set folder. If the terminal says "not recognized," it's not broken, the terminal just doesn't know where to look yet. One command tells it where. Then it works, and that's what lets you run /mcp to connect the Supabase database.

2. "It says it worked, but I can't see it, or it looks broken."

404 · "how do I see my page?" · email rate limit · "it didn't type in the terminal like the video" · push failed

Most "it's broken" moments aren't. A change on your computer isn't live until you commit and push. And some things, like Supabase's email limit, or Claude finishing a step for you, are expected, not errors. When in doubt: did I push it, and is this actually a failure or just how it works?

3. "Do I need this? / How do I add that?"

GitHub Copilot · uploading videos · what to buy

You don't need extra paid tools, your stack is set (no Copilot, we use Claude Code). And when you want to add something like videos, use the simple path: host them on YouTube as Unlisted and embed them. Don't overcomplicate it.
