Everyone is talking about using AI. The people who actually win are the ones building with it — shipping real web apps that other people pay to use, month after month. That is exactly what we do inside Leveraging AI.

It's my community for building AI-powered web apps you can charge for, even if you have never written a line of code. We don't stop at demos or ideas. We go from a blank folder to a live product you can put in front of real customers — and we do it together.

How it works

  1. Set up. Get your tools ready — the same simple setup I use to start every build.
  2. Build. Describe the app you want and let AI build it for you. No coding background required.
  3. Launch. Deploy it live to the internet so real people can actually use it.
  4. Grow. Add payments and turn it into something people subscribe to — a real business, not just a project.

Powered by the tools that make it possible — Claude to build, Supabase for your data, and Stripe to get paid.

You don't need to learn to code. You need to learn to build.
