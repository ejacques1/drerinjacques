Anthropic Is Giving Pro Users $100 to Use Fable 5 — Claim It by August 2

If you're on Claude Pro, there's $100 waiting for you to build with Anthropic's most powerful model. The catch is the clock: claim it soon, and spend it before it expires.

By Dr. Erin Jacques

July 25, 2026

Anthropic is handing Claude Pro subscribers $100 in credits to spend on Fable 5, its most powerful model. It's free money toward whatever you want to build — but the window to grab it is short, and it's already open.

Here's why it matters: if you've been wanting to build an AI-powered web app with Claude's most capable model, this covers your first $100. That's enough to research, build, and deploy something real without spending a cent of your own on the model.

How to claim your $100

  1. Make sure you're on the Pro plan.
  2. Open Claude and go to Settings → Usage.
  3. You'll see a banner for your Fable 5 credits. Click Claim.

The two dates that matter

This is time-boxed, so put both on your calendar:

  1. Claim by August 2. If you don't claim the credit by then, it's gone.
  2. Spend by September 17. Whatever you don't use by that date expires.

Free credits only count if you actually build something with them.
