Amazon Blocked Meta’s Muse. Use This Prompt So AI Shoppers Can Find You.

AI agents are starting to shop for people, and Amazon is fighting back. Here’s what that means if you sell anything online, plus a copy-paste prompt to make your offer easy for AI to find and buy.

By Dr. Erin JacquesHuman ideas, AI assist. Upfront, as always.

October 9, 2026

Amazon just blocked the No. 1 app in America from its store.

The app is Muse, Meta’s new AI agent. It books, fills out forms and buys things for you. Within weeks of launch it passed ChatGPT as the top free app on the US App Store, with 5 million downloads in 22 days.

Then people started using it to shop on Amazon, and Amazon shut it out.

Why did Amazon block Meta’s Muse?

Amazon says Muse was never authorized to shop its store. Shoppers who try now get a popup saying an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon’s Conditions of Use, according to Adweek. Amazon also says Muse doesn’t identify itself as an AI and appears to store customers’ Amazon logins.

Those are fair concerns. But there’s more at stake: Muse shops too well.

On Amazon, you search, scroll past sponsored products and get pulled into “frequently bought together.” Every one of those moments is a paid ad. An AI agent skips all of it. It finds the item, buys it and leaves.

Amazon’s ad business passed $68 billion last year. An agent that never sees an ad threatens that money, which is why Muse joins ChatGPT and Claude on the list of AI agents Amazon has blocked.

AI agents don’t scroll. When shoppers stop scrolling, the business built on the scroll starts to crack.

Dr. Erin Jacques

What does this mean if you sell online?

Buyers are starting to ask AI instead of scrolling. When someone says “find me a beginner course on building AI apps,” the agent doesn’t browse. It reads the facts it can find and picks.

That changes who wins. Businesses built on ads and impulse buys lose ground. Businesses whose offers are clear enough for an AI to understand get recommended.

If you sell a course, coaching, a digital product or a service, an AI agent may soon read your sales page before any person does.

Your move: make your offer easy for an AI to buy

  1. Ask an AI to shop for you. In ChatGPT or Muse, describe what your ideal customer would ask for. See if you show up, and what it says about you.
  2. Give AI Google’s playbook and your page. Google publishes its own guidance on product structured data: the labels that tell machines your price, what’s included and how to buy. Copy the prompt below, add your sales page link, and let AI do the work.
  3. Check the result. Run your page through Google’s free Rich Results Test to confirm it reads your offer correctly.
The prompt
Read Google's guide to product structured data:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product

Then read my sales page: [YOUR PAGE URL]

1. Write the structured data (JSON-LD) for my offer, following Google's guide. Use only facts from my page. If something Google needs is missing, list it instead of making it up.
2. Rewrite the top of my page so an AI shopping agent understands it in 10 seconds: what it is, who it's for, what's included, the price and how to buy.
3. Write 5 FAQ questions and answers my buyers actually ask.
4. Tell me exactly where to paste the code on my website.

The businesses AI agents can understand are the ones they’ll recommend. This gets you on that list early, while most people are still optimizing for the scroll.

Shopping is shifting from browsing to asking. Make sure that when someone asks, the answer is you.
