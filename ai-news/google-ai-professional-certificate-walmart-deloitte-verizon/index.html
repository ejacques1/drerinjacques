Google AI Professional Certificate: The AI Course Walmart, Deloitte and Verizon Use to Train Their Employees

No experience, no coding, about 10 hours. Here’s what it teaches, what it really costs, and how small business owners get it free.

By Dr. Erin JacquesHuman ideas, AI assist. Upfront, as always.

November 15, 2026

There’s an AI certificate from Google that can actually help you get hired, and big companies are already using it.

It’s the Google AI Professional Certificate. You don’t need experience or coding, and you can finish it in about 10 hours. Here’s why it matters, what it costs, and the fastest way to get it.

What is the Google AI Professional Certificate?

It’s Google’s hands-on AI training on Coursera, built to take you from the basics to using AI in your actual work. Google launched it in February 2026 as the next step after its AI Essentials course.

It covers using AI for planning, research, writing, content, data analysis, and building and launching an app, with more than 20 hands-on activities along the way. It’s beginner level, and no coding is needed.

Why does this certificate matter?

Because the companies doing the hiring chose it to train their own people.

In Google’s announcement, it said employers including Walmart and Sam’s Club, Deloitte, Verizon and Colgate-Palmolive will be using the certificate to train their employees on AI. Walmart is offering it to Walmart and Sam’s Club associates at no cost.

And there’s a big gap to fill. Research from Ipsos and Google found that 70% of managers believe an AI-trained workforce is critical to business success, yet only 14% of employees say they’ve been offered AI training.

How much does it cost?

It’s $49 a month on Coursera after a 7-day free trial, and most people finish in about a month.

Coursera lists the program at about 8 hours, and Google says around 10. Either way, plan for a few evenings, not a semester. According to one detailed review, it’s also included with Coursera Plus if you already pay for that.

Can you get it for free?

Yes, if you own a small business in the U.S.

Google is offering every small business no-cost access to the certificate, plus three months of Google Workspace Business Standard. It’s working with the U.S. Chamber of Commerce and America’s Small Business Development Centers to reach owners, and ADP, PayPal and Verizon are sharing the offer with their small business customers. You apply at grow.google/small-business.

What do people get wrong about it?

“I’ll finish during the free trial and pay nothing.” Don’t count on it. Coursera says you won’t receive a certificate until the trial ends and your first monthly payment goes through.

“Walmart will hire me because I have it.” That’s not what the companies said. They said they’re using it to train their employees. That tells you which AI skills they value. It isn’t a promise of a job.

“It’s the same as AI Essentials.” It isn’t. AI Essentials is Google’s lighter introduction. The Professional Certificate is the bigger, more hands-on program, at the same monthly price.

When big companies train their own people on something, that’s the skill they want in the next person they hire.

What’s changing

Knowing a little about AI used to be a nice extra on a résumé. Now employers are building AI training into how they develop their workforce, and they’re picking specific programs to do it.

That means the people who already have those skills don’t have to wait for their company to catch up.

How do you get it? Your move

  1. Ask your employer first. Some companies, like Walmart, offer it to their workers at no cost.
  2. If you own a small business, apply for free access at grow.google/small-business.
  3. Otherwise, sign up on Coursera through Google’s page at grow.google/ai-professional. Block about 10 hours on your calendar so you finish inside one month.
  4. Turn one lesson into proof. This part is my idea: when you finish, use what you learned to fix one real task at your job, then share what you did on LinkedIn. A certificate says you learned it. A real example shows you can use it.

This one helps you stand out: you’ll have the same AI training big employers chose for their own teams, plus proof you can apply it.

Your company may get around to training you eventually. You don’t have to wait.
