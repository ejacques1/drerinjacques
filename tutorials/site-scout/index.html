What if you could build an AI agent that finds businesses that need websites, builds the website for them, and then gives you the owner's contact information so you can offer it to them?

That's exactly what I'm going to show you how to build today.

And we're not talking about an automation that just follows the same steps every time.

We're building an AI agent.

You give it a goal — like finding local businesses in a particular industry that don't have websites — and the agent figures out how to complete the job.

It searches for the businesses.

It identifies which ones don't have websites.

It gathers information about the business.

It builds a website specifically for that business.

And then it gives you the information you need to contact the owner.

Show the agent working + finished website

And there's a real market behind this.

More than 6 million small businesses in America don't have a website.

One line to check

"More than 6 million" is defensible — current data puts roughly 27% of US small businesses without a website, which is closer to 9 million. But some sources say 17%, which lands just under 6 million.

Safer and stronger: "Roughly one in four small businesses in America still doesn't have a website." Better supported, and a fraction lands harder than a number that big.
