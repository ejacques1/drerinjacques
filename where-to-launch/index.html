Part 1
Before you post anywhere

You only get one first impression.

The moment you post, strangers click. If the link is broken or the page looks unfinished, they leave and they don't come back. Check these first.

  1. It's live on a real domain. Not a link that only works on your laptop, and not a random -b65p.vercel.app address.
  2. It has a privacy policy and terms. Every directory visitor is a stranger, and strangers trust sites that look legitimate. Review Protect Your App if you skipped this.
  3. It works on a phone. Most people will click your link from their phone. Open it on yours before you post.
  4. You can say what it does in one sentence. Who it's for and what it does for them. Every site will ask you for this.
  5. You have 3 screenshots or a 30-second video. People decide in seconds. Show them, don't just tell them.
  6. You can catch an email. Most visitors won't buy today. A waitlist or free signup means you can reach them tomorrow.
A change on your computer is not a change on your site.

Open your live link in a private window before you post it anywhere. That's what a stranger sees.

Part 2
Where to post it

Pick 2 or 3. Not all of them.

Each site has a different crowd and different rules. Posting the same ad everywhere doesn't work. Pick the ones that fit your app and do them well.

The rules that matter everywhere

  • Tell the story, not the ad. "I'm a teacher and I built this in 3 weeks with Claude Code" gets read. "Check out my app!" gets skipped.
  • Reply to every comment the same day. On launch day, your replies matter as much as your post.
  • Write it new for each site. Same app, different words for each crowd.
  • Save every piece of feedback. Strangers will tell you what your friends won't.

Let Claude write your listings

Run this inside your project in Claude Code, so it can read what your app actually does.

I'm about to list this app on launch sites. Read my project so you understand what it does and who it's for, then write: 1. A one-sentence description: who it's for and what it does for them. 2. A short tagline, under 60 characters. 3. A Product Hunt description, plus a first comment from me telling the story of why I built it. 4. A short listing for AI tool directories, with 3 categories it fits. Use plain language. Don't invent features or numbers my app doesn't have. Ask me questions first if anything is unclear.

Read every word before you post. You're the one putting your name on it.

Part 3
Build cheaper

Stop paying Claude to search.

When you ask Claude Code about your app, it doesn't know where anything is. So it searches: opens a file, finds it's the wrong one, opens another. All of that reading uses up your plan before any real work starts.

And everything it read stays in the conversation, so you keep paying for that search on every message after.

You don't pay for the search once. You pay for it the rest of the session.

What Graphify does

Graphify is a free tool that reads your project once and builds a map: every file, every function, and how they connect. After that, Claude checks the map first and only opens the files it needs.

How to install it

  1. Open Claude Code in your app's project folder.
  2. Paste the prompt below. Claude installs it and builds the map for you.
  3. Then just work normally. It adds a note to your CLAUDE.md telling Claude to check the map first.
Paste this into Claude Code
Install Graphify in this project: https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify Notes: the package name is "graphifyy" with two y's. It needs Python 3.10 or later, so check my version first. Explain each step in plain language before you run it. Then build the graph for this project, and show me the report it creates and how you'll use it from now on.
Only use the official repo, github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify. There are many copies on GitHub, and you don't want to install code from a stranger's copy.

Part 4
Claude does chores

A messy Google Drive, fixed in 2 prompts.

Claude can connect to your Google Drive, look at what's there, and organize it for you. First it plans, then it does the work after you approve.

Set up once

  1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors.
  2. Connect Google Drive and sign in with your Google account.

Prompt 1: look and plan (changes nothing)

Paste this into Claude
Look through my Google Drive. Don't move, rename, or delete anything yet. Tell me what's there, then suggest a simple folder structure that fits how I actually use it. Show me which files would go where, which ones look like duplicates, and which file names are unclear.

Prompt 2: do it

Paste this after you've checked the plan
Go ahead with the plan. Create the folders, move the files, and rename unclear files so I can tell what they are. Do not delete anything. Put possible duplicates and junk in a folder called "Review before deleting" so I can decide myself. When you're done, give me a short summary of what changed.
Always plan first, then act. Reading the plan before you say yes is how you stay in control of what AI does with your files.
