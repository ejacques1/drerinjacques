> Companies like Senja, Testimonial.to, and Trustmary help you do four things:
>
> - Ask customers for testimonials
> - Collect written and video responses
> - Organize and approve them
> - Display them on your website

> Those are valuable features. But depending on the platform and plan, you may pay for them every month or every year.

Show the pricing pages briefly, but avoid turning the video into a long software comparison.

## 3. Introduce your experiment

**Point:** You decided to recreate the workflow you were already paying for.

> I currently use Senja. I already have testimonials stored there, so I didn’t want to start over or lose what my customers had shared.

**Point:** Give AI a clear example.

Show the exported files and how you organized them inside the project folder.
